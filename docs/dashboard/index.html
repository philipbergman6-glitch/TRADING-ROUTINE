Provenance. All figures verbatim from origin/main of the TRADING-ROUTINE repo, latest log — always read the remote; the cloud crons push straight to main and local clones go stale within days. Everything above is regenerated from those files by scripts/build_dashboard_data.py, which hard-fails rather than publish partial or stale data; the panels marked editorial are hand-written and dated.

Known data limits — stated, not hidden. (1) Benchmark: the logged S&P series re-fetched its starting level in 3 weeks instead of chaining from the prior close, so it was discontinuous. The chart plots the re-chained series (spxc), marked *; weeks marked † logged no index levels and are carried as-is. The correction makes the S&P stronger (+5.12% vs +3.26% as logged) and the bot's relative record worse. Full detail in the Benchmark Data Errata section of WEEKLY-REVIEW.md. (2) Blotter: Apr–May exits (AMD, PLTR, MU, AVGO) predate the weekly-review era and were never logged; those rows say so rather than guess — which is why logged realized P&L is negative while the account is up. (3) Positions and stops were reconciled 1:1 against the live Alpaca API on 2026-08-11: share counts, entries and stop levels matched the log exactly. Paper Alpaca account — no real capital at risk.
